    Computational Immunology Bootcamp¶

    A 5-week hands-on introduction to computational modeling in translational immunology.

    No prior biology or programming experience required. By the end, you will be able to analyze real immune cell data from thousands of patients and build machine learning models that predict how immune cells recognize diseases.

    Who Is This For?¶

    Anyone curious about how computers can help us understand the immune system. Whether you are a high school student exploring a future career, a college student switching fields, or a professional picking up new skills -- this bootcamp will take you from zero to running real bioinformatics analyses.

    What Will You Learn?¶

    Week Topic What You Will Build
    Week 0 Computer Setup A working bioinformatics workstation
    Week 1 Immunology Foundations Your first data exploration notebook
    Week 2 Single-Cell RNA-seq Analysis Cell-type map of 2,700 blood immune cells
    Week 3 TCR & BCR Repertoire Analysis Immune receptor diversity dashboard
    Week 4 Multi-omics Integration Integrated multi-modal immune profile
    Final Capstone Project Publication-quality figures and report

    How to Use This Bootcamp¶

    The Golden Rules

    1. Go at your own pace. The "weeks" are suggested timelines. Take longer if you need to.
    2. Type every command yourself. Do not copy-paste blindly. Typing builds muscle memory.
    3. Read the error messages. They are clues, not failures. Check the Troubleshooting Guide before panicking.
    4. Use the checkpoints. Each section has checkpoints. Do not move on until you pass them.
    5. Look up words you don't know. The Glossary explains all technical terms in plain language.
    6. Ask AI for help. If you are stuck, paste your error message into ChatGPT or Claude and describe what you were trying to do. This is not cheating -- it is how professionals work.

    What You Need Before Starting¶

    • A computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux) with at least 8 GB of RAM and 20 GB of free disk space
    • A stable internet connection (for downloading software and data)
    • About 5-10 hours per week
    • Patience and curiosity

    How to Know You Are Done With Each Week¶

    Every week ends with a Self-Check section. It contains specific questions and expected outputs. If your answers and outputs match, you are ready for the next week.

    Suggested Schedule¶

    Day Activity Time
    Mon-Tue Read the background material, take notes 2-3 hrs
    Wed-Thu Work through the hands-on tutorial 3-4 hrs
    Fri Complete the self-check, review what you learned 1-2 hrs

    Weekends are for rest or catching up.

    When You Get Stuck¶

    Getting stuck is a normal part of learning -- it happens to every scientist and programmer. This bootcamp is designed to help you solve most problems on your own, because debugging is itself a valuable skill. Try these steps first:

    Self-check steps (try these before reaching out)

    1. Re-read the instructions -- Sometimes you missed a small detail
    2. Check the Troubleshooting Guide -- Your error is probably listed there
    3. Google the exact error message -- Put it in quotes for precise results
    4. Ask an AI assistant -- Paste your code and error into ChatGPT or Claude
    5. Search Bioinformatics forums -- Biostars and Stack Overflow have answers to most questions
    6. Take a break -- Sometimes the answer comes after stepping away

    Still stuck after trying the above? That's totally okay.

    You are always welcome to reach out with questions. Just include what you tried so far -- this helps us help you faster, and you will often find the answer yourself while writing it up.
